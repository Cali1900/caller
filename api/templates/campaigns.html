Campaigns

A campaign is a named configuration. There are two kinds, and the difference is not cosmetic:

typeownshow many run at once
call prompt version, cap, spacing, calling windows, email 1 copy ONE. There is one phone number and one worker, so starting a second asks you to stop the first.
drip the email sequence, its intervals, and the sender MANY. A lead's sequence belongs to the lead, not to whichever call campaign sourced it.

Type is set when a campaign is created and cannot be changed afterwards — changing it under live leads would move their whole ladder sideways.
